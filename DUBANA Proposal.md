## **OcMT3 Metallothionein from Wild Rice *Oryza coarctata*: A Candidate Gene for Salt Tolerance in Cultivated Rice**

### **Background and Rationale**

Salinity threatens rice production in South Asia (Dasgupta et al., 2018). *Oryza sativa* is a salt-sensitive glycophyte (Hoang et al., 2016), while *Oryza coarctata* is a halophyte tolerating up to 240 mM NaCl (Prusty et al., 2026\) and \>400 mM (Ishikawa et al., 2022). *Oryza sativa* is higher yielding but lacks this tolerance. Metallothionein is a key regulator in *Oryza coarctata* salt tolerance (Kumar et al., 2012). OcMT3 shares 82% homology with OsMT-I-3a (Usha et al., 2011). OcMT3 transformation into BRRI Dhan 67 has been done, but functional differences between OcMT3 and OsMT-I-3a remain underexplored (Tasnim et al., 2023).

### **Research objectives** 

Specific objectives:

1. Compare OcMT vs OsMT-I-3a in salt tolerance (50–120 mM; phenotype, gene expression, ion content) using CRISPR-Cas9 knockout of OsMT-I-3a gene.  
2. Find homologs in *Oryza coarctata* using NCBI database JAULJY000000000.1.  
3. Check MT3 stability, interaction, and Zn binding in silico.

Hypotheses:  
 H1: OcMT3 provides superior salt tolerance.  
 H2: Novel homologs exist in *Oryza coarctata*.  
 H3: OcMT3 has higher stability and Zn-binding affinity.

### **Proposed methodology**

We will perform CRISPR Cas9 mediated gene knockout of the *O. sativa* native OsMT-I-3a  gene  homologous  to the *O. coarctata* OcMT3 gene. The expression of the genes will be measured using qRT-PCR and other biochemical methods.Primers, reference genes, tissues, time points, biological replicates, and ΔΔCt method will follow standard protocols.

For the homolog detection, bioinformatics analysis will be done in-house using HMMER, MEME, Orthofinder and other tools with statistical validation. The database used will be NCBI accession JAULJY000000000.1. Proteomes, query sequence, E-value threshold, domain confirmation, phylogenetic tree, synteny, and OrthoFinder parameters are already ongoing and preliminary searches have found 7 homolog candidates. After statistical analysis is finalized they will be confirmed by PCR and sequencing. Primers, cDNA/gDNA, Sanger sequencing, and replicates are not decided yet. 

We are also performing molecular dynamics simulation using GROMACS and other tools to investigate interaction and binding differences between OcMT3 and OsMT-I-3a. 

For plant materials and growth conditions, transgenic plants will be grown in-house. Salt stress treatments will be applied between 50–120 mM. Samples and replicates will include wild type BRRI-67, wild *O. coarctata*, transgenic (both OcMT and OsMT-I-3a), and transgenic (OsMT-I-3a knockout). Statistical analysis will follow standard methods. The experimental design includes CRISPR Cas9 method, CTAB for DNA/RNA extraction, and qRT-PCR.

### **Expected outcomes**

1. Functional characterization of OcMT3 vs OsMT-I-3a.  
2. Identification of novel metallothionein homologs.  
3. Stability, interaction, and ion homeostasis comparison.

### **Significance of the proposed thesis research**

This research will narrow the gap between the halophytic Oryza coarctata OcMT3  genes functional  and genetic traits and can be used to improve the salt tolerance of commercial rice variants.  
This will also expand the  knowledge of OcMT3 gene and improve understanding of how metallothioneins influence Zn binding, protein stability, protein–protein interactions, and ion homeostasis under saline conditions.

*Oryza coarctata* being an allotetraploid (2n=4x=48) will both cause  difficulty and opportunity.
