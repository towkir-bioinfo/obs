## A. Course Registration & Enrollment

### Q1: How do I enroll in a cBLAST course?
**Answer:** Visit the cBLAST website at **https://cblast.du.ac.bd** and follow these steps:
1. Create an account on the website.
2. Go to the **"Enrollment Requirements"** section to review payment and enrollment guidelines.
3. Fill out the **"Register/Re-enroll a Course"** form for the course you wish to enroll in.
4. Complete the payment (bank transfer or bKash).
5. Ensure your registration details and account information are correct.

*— Based on reply to Anhaa Aziz (2026-08-05)*

---

## B. Course Access & Technical Issues

### Q7: I enrolled but my dashboard shows no courses. What should I do?
**Answer:** Don't worry — this is usually a display issue. The **"Recently Accessed Courses"** section only updates once you click into a course from your **"My Courses"** list. If you can see the course under **"My Courses"**, your enrollment is active. Simply click the course to open it, and the Recently Accessed section will update on its own.

If the issue persists, contact cBLAST at **cblastbmbdu@gmail.com**.

*— Based on reply to Mahzabeen Maruf (2026-08-05)*

---

### Q8: How long do I have access to a course?
**Answer:** Course access is typically granted for **3 months** from the date of enrollment. For Combo-2 (C6+C9), you get **5 months** to complete both courses. After the deadline, you will lose access, so download any recordings you may need before then.

*— Based on replies to Sanjina Alam Maliha (2025-12-08) and Md. Rabbi Rahul (2026-07-16)*

## D. Certificates & Completion

### Q15: When will I receive my certificate after completing a course?
**Answer:** Certificates are issued after the course deadline has passed and all assignments are graded. The timeline is:
1. Course deadline passes.
2. Assignments are graded.
3. Certificates are printed and signed.
4. You are notified via email when the certificate is ready.

Certificates are typically distributed in batches (e.g., all certificates distributed in July 2019). If you completed a course recently, you may need to wait until the next distribution cycle.

*— Based on replies to Shaheen Alam (2019-10-15) and Sanjina Alam Maliha (2026-06-11)*

## E. Assignments & Assessments

### Q18: What happens if I don't complete an assignment properly?
**Answer:** If an assignment is incomplete (e.g., MaxQuant analysis not completed), cBLAST will:
1. Notify you of the issue.
2. Give you **one additional week** to complete and resubmit.
3. If not resubmitted within that time, the assignment will receive a **score of zero**, which may affect your eligibility for the certificate.

If you face technical problems during software setup, contact cBLAST **before the deadline** for help.

*— Based on reply to Sharmin Aktar (2026-08-17)*

---
