# Prior-Art / Novelty Assessment and Seed Reading List: Metallothionein (MT) Gene Family in *Oryza coarctata*

## TL;DR
- The idea is still novel and publishable: no genome-wide/family-wide MT identification exists for *Oryza coarctata* / *Porteresia coarctata*, and the user's belief that PcMT3 (Usha et al., 2011) is the only characterised MT for this species is confirmed by the literature searched.
- The single largest scoop risk is not a dedicated MT paper but incidental MT loci buried in supplementary annotation tables of the newer O. coarctata genome (Zhao et al., 2023) and the Bansal et al. (2021) / Mondal et al. (2018) resources — this must be checked manually before claiming absolute novelty.
- Novelty depends on framing the work as the first allotetraploid-halophyte (KKLL) MT family study under combined salinity-plus-submergence stress, and on retaining the differentiating layers (Cys-motif subclassification + promoter cis-elements + tandem/segmental duplication + co-stress DESeq2 expression) that the closest precedent (Cheng et al., 2021, IJMS) did not perform for this species.

## Key Findings
1. **PcMT3 is the only characterised MT in this species.** Usha et al. (2011, *Biologia Plantarum*) cloned a single type-3 MT (PcMT3; 581-bp cDNA, 64-aa protein, 82% identity to rice OsMT-I-3a). The paper explicitly reports that "analysis of genomic clone of PcMT3 revealed the presence of two introns," that "transcript profiling for PcMT3 in P. coarctata leaves in the presence of Cd, Cu and Zn showed an increase in transcript accumulation," and that "Southern analysis suggested the presence of more than one copy of PcMT3-like sequences in the P. coarctata genome." The Southern-blot note is important: the authors already suspected additional MT loci, which the user's family study can now resolve. No family-wide study exists.
2. **The most directly competing paper deliberately excludes O. coarctata.** Cheng et al. (2021) "identified 53 MT genes from six Oryza genera, including O. sativa ssp. japonica, O. rufipogon, O. sativa ssp. indica, O. nivara, O. glumaepatula, and O. barthii," and reported "five pairs of segmental duplication events and ten pairs of tandem duplication events." The KKLL halophyte is not among the taxa. This both supplies the methodological template and leaves the coarctata niche open.
3. **The premise is functionally well-supported.** MTs are salinity-responsive, not only heavy-metal-responsive, in rice and other monocots/halophytes — e.g. Kumar et al. (2012, BMC Plant Biology) showed that "clustered metallothionein genes are co-regulated in rice and ectopic expression of OsMT1e-P confers multiple abiotic stress tolerance in tobacco via ROS scavenging," and comparable salinity-MT precedent exists in the halophyte *Salicornia brachiata* (SbMT-2), *Halostachys caspica* (HcMT) and *Leymus chinensis* (LcMT3). An expression-and-promoter salt-stress angle is therefore biologically defensible.
4. **The plant gene-family literature (maize, cassava, oat, Brassica, kiwifruit) supplies a copy-ready Methods pipeline**, but none substitutes for a coarctata-specific study.

### Prose verdict
The idea remains novel. Across the indexed literature there is (i) exactly one characterised MT gene in this species (PcMT3; Usha et al., 2011) and (ii) a genus-wide MT study that pointedly excluded O. coarctata. No dedicated genome-wide/family-wide MT identification for O. coarctata/P. coarctata was found in 2023–2026 journals or on bioRxiv. To keep the novelty defensible the manuscript must be framed not as "MT genes in another Oryza" but as **the first MT gene family characterisation in an allotetraploid (KKLL) halophytic wild rice, exploiting a genome that resolves the K and L subgenomes (Zhao et al., 2023) and a co-stress design (salinity × submergence) unavailable to the AA-genome studies.** The publishable delta over Cheng et al. (2021) is the union of: Cys-motif Type 1–4 subclassification with the halophyte-specific PcMT3 anchor; homoeolog/subgenome partitioning of MT loci (a feature only an allotetraploid offers); tandem + segmental duplication and Ka/Ks in the KKLL context; promoter cis-element cataloguing tied to salt/ABA/MYB elements; and DESeq2 differential expression across Control/Salt450/Salt700/Submerged/Salt450+Submerged. Without those differentiating layers the work risks being read as a coarctata-shaped replicate of the 2021 genus study; with them it is a distinct contribution.

**Inference boundary:** Absence of evidence in indexed literature is not proof that no group is working on this, and keyword search does not reach unindexed supplementary tables. The genuine residual risk is that the Zhao et al. (2023), Bansal et al. (2021) or Mondal et al. (2018) supplements already list MT loci in a functional-annotation dump. The user should manually inspect those supplementary annotation files before writing "first report," and phrase the claim as "first dedicated genome-wide identification and characterisation."

### Gap statement (drop-in for the manuscript introduction)
"Although metallothioneins are established multifunctional players in metal homeostasis, ROS scavenging and salinity tolerance across plants, and although the MT gene family has been catalogued genome-wide in *Oryza sativa* and five other AA-genome *Oryza* species (53 genes total; Cheng et al., 2021), the family has never been systematically identified or characterised in the only halophytic member of the genus, the allotetraploid (KKLL) wild rice *Oryza coarctata*. To date, a single MT (PcMT3) has been cloned from this species, and even that study inferred — by Southern blot — the presence of additional, uncharacterised PcMT3-like loci. The complement, subgenomic distribution, Cys-motif subclassification, promoter architecture, duplication history and salinity/submergence-responsive expression of O. coarctata MT genes remain uncharacterised."

## Recommendations
1. **Before writing "first report," manually inspect the supplementary annotation tables** of Zhao et al. (2023), Bansal et al. (2021) and Mondal et al. (2018) for any pre-listed MT / Metallothio_2 / PF01439 loci. Threshold: if any supplement already tabulates the full MT complement, downgrade the claim to "first dedicated characterisation" and pivot novelty to the functional/co-stress layers.
2. **Re-anchor the analysis on the 2023 chromosome-level genome, not only Pcoar_v1.1.** The user's 7 IDs derive from the fragmented Pcoar_v1.1; re-running hmmsearch/blastp against Zhao et al. (2023) enables true chromosomal mapping, subgenome (K vs L) assignment and MCScanX synteny — the features that most differentiate this work from the 2021 genus study. If homoeolog pairs resolve cleanly across K/L, make subgenome partitioning a headline result.
3. **Cross-validate candidate count against the AA-genome benchmark.** Cheng et al. (2021) found 7–12 MT genes per AA-genome species. The user's 7 unique IDs sit at the low end; for an allotetraploid this is a red flag for under-recovery (consistent with the collapsed/fragmented Pcoar_v1.1). Expect roughly double the diploid count (~14–24) if both subgenomes retain MTs. If only 7 are recovered, test for assembly collapse/homoeolog merging before interpreting the number biologically.
4. **Frame expression around salinity as well as heavy metal.** Lead with the salinity-MT precedent (rice OsMT1e-P, Kumar et al., 2012; halophyte SbMT-2; Halostachys HcMT; Leymus LcMT3) so that MT up-regulation under salt/submergence reads as biologically expected, not anomalous — and connect it mechanistically to ROS scavenging (Hassinen et al., 2011).
5. **Keep the differentiating analytical stack non-negotiable:** Cys-motif Type 1–4 subclassification (calibrated to PcMT3 and the seven Arabidopsis MTs), promoter cis-elements, tandem + segmental duplication with Ka/Ks, and DESeq2 across all five conditions. Dropping any one narrows the delta over Cheng et al. (2021).

## Caveats
- Absence of a competing MT-family paper is established only within indexed, English-language literature reachable by keyword search as of September 2026; it does not exclude in-progress or unindexed work, nor MT loci hidden in supplementary annotation dumps.
- The genus-wide precedent (Cheng et al., 2021) is close enough methodologically that reviewers will demand explicit justification for a separate coarctata paper; the halophyte biology + allotetraploid subgenome + co-stress design is that justification and must be argued, not assumed.
- The user's current 7-gene set is derived from the fragmented Pcoar_v1.1 assembly; gene count and duplication inferences are provisional until re-run on the chromosome-level 2023 genome.
- A few references above (Hassinen et al., 2011; Sengupta & Majumder, 2010; the exact IQ-TREE 2 and Pfam release details) were verified via secondary citation context or general knowledge of the tool versions rather than the primary article page in this session; confirm volume/page/DOI against the publisher before final submission.
- The Mekawy et al. (2018) rice salinity-MT paper concerns OsMT-3a (type 3), not OsMT1a; if cited, use OsMT-3a (type 3, *Environmental and Experimental Botany* 147:157–166) and reserve OsMT1a for Yang et al. (2009, *Plant Molecular Biology* 70:219–229). This distinction is easy to get wrong in the introduction.
