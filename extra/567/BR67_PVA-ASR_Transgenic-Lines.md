## Notes

- **Nomenclature:** BGround - TransGene - MUH number
  - BR75 = *Oryza sativa* var. BR75 background
  - BR81 = *Oryza sativa* var. BR81 background
  - Oe ASR1 = Overexpression of *Oryza* ASR1 gene (Abscisic Acid and Stress Response)
  - Oe PVA1 = Overexpression of *Oryza* PVA1 gene (Phenylvalinate oxidase or similar)
  - MUH = Lab numbering system for individual lines

- **Generation tracking:** T1, T4 generations noted; verify which are seed-set vs. segregating

- **P-numbers:** Likely refer to plot numbers (P) and sub-replicates (e.g., P-52-3 = Plot 52, replicate 3)

- **Status:** Most lines show "T1 seeds available in PBT" — store location is the PBT facility
