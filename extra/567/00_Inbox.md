# Inbox

Everything unsorted lands here first. Capture fast, don't organize — that happens later.

When you process an entry: give it a `type`, move its content into the right home (`01_Projects`, `02_Areas`, `03_Resources`), and delete the line below.

## Where things go once processed

| If it's... | It goes to... |
|---|---|
| A project task, decision, or log entry | `01_Projects/<project>/Log.md` or the project's own notes |
| An ongoing responsibility (lab, degree, admin, life) | `02_Areas/` |
| A recurring task or OKR | `02_Areas/Task-System/` |
| A paper, atomic concept, method, protocol, person, meeting, or dated note | `03_Resources/` |
| Dead, superseded, or no-longer-relevant material | `99_Archives/` |
