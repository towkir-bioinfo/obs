## 4. Notes on the data

- Several private universities (NSU, BRAC) don't publish individual department
  office emails — only faculty names and a web contact form. Sending to the
  general admissions/registrar address with "Attn: [Department name]" in the
  subject line is the functional workaround.
- "Data Science" as a standalone department barely exists in Dhaka; it's almost
  always folded into CSE, except DU's IASDS/ISRT.
- Student-run subject clubs (e.g., EWU's Biotech Club) are frequently faster for
  actual poster placement than the faculty office, since they run their own
  event/notice boards.
- Chairpersons and office emails rotate yearly — verify each address against the
  department's live contact page immediately before sending, not from this list
  alone.
