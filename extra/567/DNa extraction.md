# Plant Genomic DNA Extraction by CTAB Method

## Reagents and Consumables

|Reagent|Working Concentration / Volume|
|---|---|
|CTAB extraction buffer|5 mL per 1 g plant tissue|
|β-mercaptoethanol|10 µL per 5 mL CTAB buffer|
|Phenol:chloroform:isoamyl alcohol (PCI)|25:24:1 (v/v/v)|
|Chloroform:isoamyl alcohol (CI)|24:1 (v/v)|
|Ice-cold isopropanol|—|
|Ice-cold 99% ethanol|—|
|70% ethanol (ice-cold)|—|
|3 M sodium acetate (NaOAc)|—|
|TE buffer (10 mM Tris-HCl, 1 mM EDTA, pH 8.0)|—|
|DNase-free RNase A|10 µL per 1 mL TE (scale proportionally)|
|Liquid nitrogen|—|

## Equipment Notes

- Water bath set to 60°C. Water level must be sufficient to submerge the entire reaction mixture in each tube.
- For Falcon tubes: centrifuge at 4000–6000 rpm.
- For Eppendorf tubes: centrifuge at 10,000 rpm.
- Collect as much leaf tissue as possible per sample.

---

## Day 1 — Lysis and Phase Separation

1. Wear gloves throughout. Add 10 µL β-mercaptoethanol to 5 mL CTAB extraction buffer in a test tube. Add β-mercaptoethanol slowly by touching the wall of the tube. Mix thoroughly. Repeat for each tube.
    
2. Grind plant leaves to a fine powder in a mortar and pestle under liquid nitrogen.
    
3. Using a spatula, transfer the powdered tissue into the CTAB buffer pre-warmed to 60°C in the water bath.
    
4. Incubate at 60°C for 30 min. Mix gently by inversion every 5 min.
    
5. Add an equal volume (5 mL) of PCI (25:24:1). Wrap the PCI bottle in aluminium foil to protect from light. Shake before dispensing to each tube. Mix gently by inversion. Allow to stand for 1 min after addition.
    
6. Equalise tube weights with PCI. Mix by gentle inversion.
    
7. Centrifuge at 4000 rpm for 15–20 min (or 10,000 rpm for Eppendorf tubes).
    
8. Carefully transfer the upper aqueous phase (~5–6 mL) into a new tube. Avoid disturbing the interphase.
    
9. Add 2/3 volume of ice-cold isopropanol slowly by touching the wall of the tube.
    
10. Mix gently by inversion to precipitate the DNA.
    
11. Incubate overnight at −20°C.
    

---

## Day 2 — Washing and Pellet Recovery

1. Remove tubes from −20°C. Allow to equilibrate at room temperature for 10–15 min.
    
2. Centrifuge at 4000 rpm for 20 min. Ensure tubes are balanced.
    
3. Carefully discard the supernatant without disturbing the pellet.
    
4. Wash the pellet with 70% ice-cold ethanol.
    
5. Balance tubes.
    
6. Centrifuge at 4000 rpm for 10 min.
    
7. Discard the supernatant. Invert the tube on tissue paper and allow the pellet to air-dry completely.
    
8. Dissolve the pellet in ice-cold TE buffer (1–2 mL, scale to expected DNA yield).
    
9. Incubate overnight at 4°C to allow complete dissolution.
    

---

## Day 3 — RNase Treatment and Purification

1. Add DNase-free RNase A (for 2 mL TE: 100 µL RNase; for 1 mL TE: 10 µL RNase; scale proportionally — 0.2 µL RNase per 100 µL DNA solution).
    
2. Incubate at 37°C for 20–30 min.
    
3. Add an equal volume of PCI (25:24:1). Mix gently by inversion.
    
4. Centrifuge at 4000 rpm for 15 min. Transfer the aqueous phase to Eppendorf tubes (~600 µL per tube).
    
5. Add an equal volume of PCI. Centrifuge at 10,000 rpm for 15 min.
    
6. Transfer the aqueous phase to new Eppendorf tubes (~500 µL per tube).
    
7. Add an equal volume of chloroform:isoamyl alcohol (24:1). Centrifuge at 10,000 rpm for 15 min. Transfer the aqueous phase to new Eppendorf tubes (~400 µL per tube).
    
8. Add 1/10th volume of 3 M sodium acetate and 2.5 volumes of ice-cold 99% ethanol. Mix gently.
    
9. Incubate overnight at −20°C.
    

---

## Day 4 — Final Wash and Resuspension

1. Centrifuge at 10,000 rpm for 15 min. Discard the supernatant.
    
2. Wash the pellet with 1 mL 70% ice-cold ethanol.
    
3. Centrifuge at 10,000 rpm for 15 min.
    
4. Discard the supernatant. Air-dry the pellet. If the starting tissue amount was large, repeat the wash: add 1 mL 70% ice-cold ethanol → centrifuge at 10,000 rpm for 15 min → discard supernatant → dry.
    
5. Dissolve the pellet in 50 µL TE buffer.
    
6. Store at −20°C for long-term storage.

## Abbreviated 2-Day Variant (As Performed in Our Lab)

### Day 1

- Complete Day 1 steps 1–8 as above.
- Immediately add RNase A to the TE buffer, incubate at 37°C for 30 min.
- Add 1/10th volume 3 M sodium acetate and 2/3 volume ice-cold isopropanol.
- Incubate overnight at −20°C.

### Day 2

- Follow Day 2 steps 1–8.
- Continue with Day 3 step 3 onward (PCI purification rounds) for higher purity.

### Optional Additional Purification

After the final PCI extraction (Day 3, step 7), an additional chloroform:isoamyl alcohol (24:1) extraction and centrifugation step can be performed before ethanol precipitation for improved purity.
