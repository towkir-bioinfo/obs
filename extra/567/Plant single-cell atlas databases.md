# Plant single-cell atlas databases

How to check whether a single-cell atlas already exists for a given plant species — you do not need to guess. Groups upload maps to centralised databases and registries.

## 1. Dedicated plant single-cell databases

| Resource | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| [scPlantDB](https://bio.tools/scplantdb) | Most comprehensive; integrates millions of cells across dozens of plant species |
| [Plant Single Cell Browser](https://www.zmbp-resources.uni-tuebingen.de/timmermans/plant-single-cell-browser/) | Tissue-specific queries (leaf, root) via lab-maintained portal |
| [Plant sc-Atlas](https://bioit3.irc.ugent.be/plant-sc-atlas/) | Development-focused visualisation, detailed for model organisms |
| [Plant Single Cell Transcriptome Hub (PsctH)](https://www.omicsempower.com/blog/plant-single-cell-sequencing-cell-type-annotation-databases-guide/) | Marker genes + existing datasets |
| [Plant Cell Atlas](https://www.plantcellatlas.org/) | Community initiative portal |

## 2. Global initiatives

- [The Plant Cell Atlas Initiative](https://www.plantcellatlas.org/) — published datasets and community updates; shows which crops/wild plants are prioritised
- [The Biodiversity Cell Atlas](https://biodiversitycellatlas.org/) — maps the tree of life at single-cell resolution, includes non-model plants

## 3. Targeted keyword search

Atlas technology moves fast; newly completed atlases may only exist in recent papers. Search Google Scholar / PubMed with:

```
"[Scientific or Common Name]" AND "single-cell atlas" OR "scRNA-seq"
```

Applied to this project:

```
"Oryza coarctata" AND "single-cell atlas" OR "scRNA-seq"
```

**Result: no *O. coarctata* single-cell atlas exists.** See [[Oryza coarctata data inventory]].
