## 1. Netosis & Antitumor Activity

### Core Concept
**Netosis** — a form of cell death involving the release of neutrophil extracellular traps (NETs).

### Phases of Netosis

| Phase | Key Mediators |
|-------|---------------|
| **Initiation** | ROS, Elastase |
| **Proliferation** | IRS1, AKT, GCSF, MMP9 |
| **Remodelling** | MMP8, MMP9 |

### Associated Pathways

- **Pathway 1** — `activation`
- **Pathway 2**
- **Pathway 3**

### Key Markers / Labels Mentioned

- **LipZic**
- **GPb**
- **4C**
- **Man city new**

### Apoptosis-Related Proteins

> **BAX:** Bcl-2-Associated X protein
> **BAK:** Bcl-2 Associated Killer

## 2. Immune Cell Interactions in the Tumor Microenvironment

### Immune Cell Types Involved

- **APC** (Antigen Presenting Cell)
- **CTL** (Cytotoxic T Lymphocyte)
- **Memory T Cell**
- **T-reg** (Regulatory T Cell)
- **Th17** — associated with *inflammation*
- **B-cell**

### Key Interaction: NK-Mediated Cytotoxicity

> **NK cells kill and protect**

### Cell Differentiation & Signaling Flow

```
Naive T Cell
   │
   ├── differentiate into ──> Effector T Cells (CTL & Th1 cell)
   ├── differentiate into ──> Regulatory T Cell (Treg)
   ├── differentiate into ──> Th2 Cell
   │
Conditioned Dendritic Cell (Immunosuppressive)
   │
   └── promotes differentiation ──> Effector T Cells (CTL & Th1 cell)
                                    Regulatory T Cell (Treg)
                                    Th2 Cell
```

### Tumor-Promoting Cells & Factors

| Cell / Factor | Role |
|---------------|------|
| **M2 macrophage** | Tumor-associated, promotes growth |
| **VEGF** | Growth & angiogenic factor |
| **MMP** | Matrix metalloproteinases |
| **MDSC** | Myeloid-derived suppressor cell |
| **ROS** | Reactive oxygen species |
| **NF-kB** | Transcription factor |
| **TNF, IL-6, IL-10** | Cytokines |

### Signaling Actions

- **Secretes signals**
- **Conditions**
- **Differentiate into**
- **Promote differentiation**
- **Block** (×3)
- **Release** (×2)
- **Stimulates**
- **Induces**

### Downstream Effects

- **Tumor Cell Proliferation**
- **New Blood Vessel Formation**
- **Growth & Angiogenic Factors** *(e.g., VEGF)*
