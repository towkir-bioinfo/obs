# OS-E001 — PCR confirmation of MT gene in T3 transgenic plants

## Question

Is the MT transgene actually present in each of the 25 T3-generation BR67 plants?

## Why it matters

Due to Mendelian inheritance, the MT gene may not transmit from one generation to the next. Every downstream phenotyping or expression claim assumes the transgene is present — that assumption has to be verified per line before anything else is run.

## Material

- **Plants:** 25 individuals, T3 generation
- **Background:** BR67 rice
- **Tissue:** young leaf
- Line registry: [[02_knocout_os_br67_mt_plant_lines_registry]]

## Method

1. Genomic DNA extraction — [[02_knocout_os_ctab_dna_extraction_protocol]]
2. PCR for the MT transgene
3. Gel electrophoresis to score presence/absence per plant

## Result

> [!warning] DNA quality
> The DNA mix did not come out good. One candidate cause: the CTAB buffer was not shaken at step 3 of the protocol.
